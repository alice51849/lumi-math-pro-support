1. What it is

Lumi Math Pro: Kids Numbers is the paid-download edition of a picture-based math app that trains thinking: 46 question types, every one read aloud, with weak-spot practice and printable worksheets.

  • 46 question types covering patterns, logic, word problems and geometry, plus addition, subtraction, multiplication and division
  • Weak-spot practice that brings back the question types a child misses most, with new numbers
  • Five skill scores in the Progress tab: numbers and operations, patterns, logic, geometry and real-life application
  • Parent Zone worksheets: 20 questions with an answer key, to print or save as a PDF, plus a one-page learning report
  • Daily challenge, a 60-second race, and 12 badges and 14 space friends to collect

2. Who it is for

Made for ages 5 to 8, and also fine for older students and adults who like logic puzzles. Pre-readers can play because every question is picture-based and read aloud, with a slow-speech option. Parents get a Parent Zone behind a parent check.

3. How it is paid for

This is a paid download. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads, and everything is included after the purchase.
